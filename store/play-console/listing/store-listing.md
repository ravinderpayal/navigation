## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Plan electric road trips across India and arrive without range anxiety.

Vinfast Copilot plans your drive, picks the charging stops, and keeps checking them as you go. Tell it your battery level, and it handles the rest: the route, when to charge, where, and for how long.

PLANNED AROUND YOUR CAR
• Enter your car's consumption at 80 km/h, battery size, rated range (ARAI or EPA) and drag coefficient once
• Estimates account for road type, traffic, stop-start town driving, air conditioning, wind and air temperature along the route
• Charging times follow a realistic charging curve, so stops above 75% aren't underestimated

SMART CHARGING STOPS
• Free chargers from the VinFast free charging list are preferred
• Available 180 kW and faster chargers right on your route are put first
• A quick 5–10 minute top-up near halfway is suggested when it's all you need
• Plans for 55% on arrival on long trips, so there's no hunt for a charger in city traffic
• Never plans to arrive anywhere below 20%

LIVE CHARGER CHECKS
• Where charging networks report status, stops are checked before they're suggested, and dead chargers are left out
• Stations near where your battery will reach 30% are monitored every few minutes during the drive
• See guns free, power, expected charge on arrival, charge time and highly rated restaurants nearby
• Rank chargers with or without food stops

TURN-BY-TURN NAVIGATION
• Google Maps navigation with voice guidance and live traffic
• The battery estimate updates from how you're actually driving: speed, braking and time standing
• A chime and vibration before the charger turn-off, so you don't miss it
• Suggests the cruising speed that gets you there soonest, charging time included
• Works with Android Auto

GOOD TO KNOW
• Battery levels are estimates. The app has no connection to your car, so update the charge after charging or when it drifts.
• Charger status is only as current as each network's own reports.
• Always follow road signs, speed limits and local laws.

Vinfast Copilot is an independent app and is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with VinFast.
