Vinfast Copilot privacy policy

Last updated: [date you publish this]

Vinfast Copilot ("the app") plans electric vehicle trips and navigates them. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and why. The app has no user accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not show ads.

Information the app uses

  • Precise location. Used to plan your route from where you are, to navigate, and to estimate battery use as you drive. It's used while you're planning or navigating, including when the app runs in the background during navigation.
  • Destination searches. The text you type to find a destination.
  • Car details and preferences. Battery level, battery size, consumption, rated range, drag coefficient, charging limit and settings such as the food preference. These stay on your device.

Who receives it

To work, the app sends some of this information to the following services. Each handles it under its own privacy policy.

  • Google (Maps Platform: Navigation SDK, Places API and Routes API): your location, the route and your destination searches, to find places, compute routes and give turn-by-turn guidance. Google's Navigation SDK also collects navigation data, such as GPS location and the route taken, to improve Google Maps, as shown in the terms you accept in the app. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo: points along your route, to get the current weather there. See the Open-Meteo terms and privacy.
  • Charging networks (Statiq and chargeindia.com): the area your route covers and the charging stations near it, to check whether chargers are working and free.

The app does not sell your information, and it does not share it for advertising.

What's stored

Car details and preferences are stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app's developer does not receive or store your location, routes or searches.

Permissions

  • Location: for routing, navigation and the battery estimate.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps navigation running when the screen is off or another app is open.
  • Vibration: alerts before a charger turn-off and when the battery is low.
  • Internet: to reach the services above.

Children

The app is meant for drivers and isn't directed at children.
